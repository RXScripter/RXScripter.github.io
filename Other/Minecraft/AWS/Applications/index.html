📜 Server Rules (AWS SMP)

1. No cheating or exploiting
No hacks, clients, dupes, X-ray, bug abuse, or anything unfair.

2. Be respectful
No toxicity, slurs, bullying, or harassment.

3. No spawn killing / griefing
No trapping spawn, repeated killing, or griefing bases.

4. No stealing from active players
Ask before taking items. Unclaimed abandoned stuff is fine.

5. PvP only if BOTH sides agree
No random killing.

6. No excessive lag machines
Redstone is okay—just don’t crash the server.

7. Keep builds appropriate
No NSFW, offensive, or hateful structures.

8. Respect staff decisions
If staff asks you to stop something, stop.

9. Have fun & keep the SMP chill
Be friendly and enjoy the world.
